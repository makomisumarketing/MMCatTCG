What we process

When we use social platform APIs (for example TikTok, Meta / Facebook, or Instagram), we may process:

  • Authorization data — API tokens and related identifiers needed for accounts we connect, stored in our own environment (for example configuration on machines we control).

  • Operational logs — local or server logs (errors, timestamps) that help us keep our automation reliable.

We do not sell personal information. We do not operate this stack as a multi-tenant consumer product.
