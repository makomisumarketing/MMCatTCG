This policy describes how MMCat TCG Uploader (“we”, “us”) handles information when you use our software or related services. Our site: https://mmcattcg.makomisumarketing.com.

What we do

MMCat TCG Uploader helps schedule and publish content to social platforms you connect, such as Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok. You control which accounts you authorize.

Information we process

Depending on how you use the app, we may process:

  • Account and authorization data — for example, OAuth tokens or identifiers needed for APIs you enable (e.g. Meta, TikTok).
  • Content you choose to post — such as video files, captions, titles, thumbnails, and scheduling metadata stored on your systems or in files you manage.
  • Technical logs — basic operational information on devices you run the software on (for example error messages or timestamps) if you enable logging.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use it for advertising on your behalf beyond what you initiate through connected platforms.

Where data lives

Credentials and content are primarily stored where you run the software (for example in configuration files or folders on your computer or server), unless you choose to store them elsewhere. We do not operate a consumer “account database” for this project unless you separately deploy one.

Third-party services

When you connect Facebook, Instagram, or TikTok, those companies process data under their own policies:

  • Meta Privacy Policy
  • TikTok Privacy Policy

Posting and scheduling happen through their APIs according to the permissions you grant.

Retention

You can delete tokens, local files, and scheduled data at any time by removing them from your environment. If we host only this static policy page, we do not collect visitor analytics through this page unless you add that later (if you add analytics, update this section).

Security

You are responsible for protecting API tokens, passwords, and devices that run the software. Do not commit secrets to public repositories.

Children

This software is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

International users

If you use connected social platforms globally, your information may be processed in countries where those providers operate.
